# Olivia Arcana — easier reading journey

September 25, 2026

## Experience changes
- Question-first entry with an explained one-card or three-card recommendation. The reader can change it, and deeper member formats remain visible under format options.
- Optional example questions insert only when clicked. Question help applies an approved direction in place, without prematurely launching a draw.
- Topic, reversals and extra ways to begin use progressive disclosure.
- Moving from single-card entry to spreads or back carries the question and preferences.
- The meaning appears when a card is revealed. Existing first impressions remain readable, but a new observation never gates the interpretation.
- Full spread synthesis is visible after the last reveal. It no longer requires chapter-by-chapter continuation.
- Saving requires no written reflection. Notes and future follow-up are optional.
- Explicit AI consent before drawing; one authorization per reading, consumed on reveal. No journal notes are sent. Unavailable AI does not block the reading.
- Secondary refinements: visual physical-card selection, simpler first-use Journey and Today, searchable living deck, guided symbol comparisons.
