# Olivia Arcana — artwork and delivery notes

The finished [single-file HTML](olivia-arcana.html) embeds Three.js, fonts, and all card artwork. It works offline. The beveled card bodies, stack, lighting, camera, and unfolding motion are rendered in 3D. The detailed porcelain motifs are generated illustrations applied as surface textures with a subtle bump response; they are not fully modeled sculptural faces.

Seven artwork families support the twenty-two reading identities. The opening Sun uses a dedicated portrait image; the other families use a coherent atlas. The dark card reverse retains the authored engraving design.

## Atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production texture atlas for eight luxury sculptural tarot cards, to be mapped onto real 3D card surfaces. Create one exceptionally beautiful high-resolution flat orthographic atlas with EXACTLY FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows, no gutters, no perspective, no cast shadow outside each panel. Overall aspect 4:3; ideally 3072x2304 or highest resolution. Every panel is portrait proportion 2:3, full-bleed warm ivory/off-white matte porcelain. There must be no text, numbers, letters, typography, logos, borders or mockup framing anywhere. Each panel has its entire central sculptural motif safely within its middle 68% width and middle 64% height, leaving top 17% and bottom 19% almost bare ivory for typesetting later. Render the art with extraordinary museum-grade neoclassical porcelain bas-relief craftsmanship, intricate hairline intaglio engraving, very subtle platinum and pale champagne inlay, velvety ivory mineral grain, deep precise recessed cuts, delicate high points, restrained warm studio grazing light from upper left. Low physical relief, not a tall statue. No plastic, no simplistic vector icons, no obvious computer primitives, no thick geometric borders. Tone: collectible art object, sculpted antique myth with contemporary editorial restraint. The relief should have exquisite detailed draughtsmanship and quiet drama. CELL ORDER left-to-right top row then bottom row: 1 SUN — poetic androgynous classical human face, eyes closed, sculpted ivory disc surrounded by a magnificent corona of delicately carved wavy rays and microscopic engraved radial lines, finely modelled lips and nose, reverent and powerful. 2 HIGH PRIESTESS — a draped feminine bust in profile partly hidden by an architectural arched veil, fine flowing carved pleats and two slender fluted columns, moon sliver behind her. 3 MOON — a large slender crescent containing a dreamy sculpted classical face in profile on its inner edge, shallow concentric ripples and one tiny pearl star, beautiful broad negative space. 4 STAR — an exquisite eight-pointed elongated star with finely fluted tapered blades, one small sculpted open hand reaching up from beneath, hairline constellation arcs and seven tiny engraved stars. 5 MAGICIAN — an elegant slender classical sculpted hand poised upright, index finger raised, holding an open delicate orbital circle above the fingertips, fine flowing drapery at wrist, tiny platinum detail. 6 WORLD — a sculpted oval wreath of extremely fine overlapping olive leaves surrounding a miniature elegant standing draped feminine figure, open airy architecture, not a dense stamp. 7 FOOL — a graceful small classical figure in loose flowing drapery stepping across an incomplete crescent-shaped stone path, one tiny carved bird rising near the shoulder, fine wind curves, sense of beginning. 8 BACK — sculpted ornamental back design only: two exquisite intertwined narrow oval ribbons woven from fine engraved botanical threads, perfectly centred, no letters or initials, warm ivory background same as every other panel. Make all eight panels coherent in scale, material and engraving sophistication; each recognizably different. Exact regular 4x2 atlas grid is critical. This is the finished surface artwork, not a website, not a photo of a deck, no outer background.

## Sun refinement prompt

The atlas above was supplied as the reference image.

Edit target: the TOP LEFT SUN artwork from this atlas. Create a single standalone high-resolution PORTRAIT 2:3 image of ONLY that Sun panel, reproducing its precise art direction and classical androgynous sculpted face, closed eyes and complex radiating wavy hair / sun rays. This is a flat, straight-on orthographic porcelain bas-relief texture, not a photographed card or mockup. Preserve the warm ivory, delicate champagne accents, exceptionally fine carved hair and engraved rays, soft raking light from the upper left and pristine matte mineral surface. Refine facial anatomy and incredibly fine lines at portrait resolution. Very important: all sculptural artwork must fit entirely inside the central 74% width and central 65% height, with quiet bare porcelain above and below; no cropping of rays. Background full bleed continuous warm ivory porcelain, uniform around the outer 15% edges. No text, numerals, labels, borders or other panels. Highest available portrait resolution.

## Validation

Syntax and browser runtime checks passed with no console errors or network dependencies. All twenty-two readings were checked at four viewport sizes. Desktop and mobile scroll motion, reverse scrolling, reduced-motion idle behavior, WebGL fallback, failed image decoding, and context recovery were checked. The final HTML is approximately 2.69 MB.
