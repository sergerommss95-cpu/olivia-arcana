Branch `claude/peaceful-clarke-scrh06`, on top of the v4 mobile pass. Not deployed to production. Netlify published it as the deploy preview of draft PR #4: https://deploy-preview-4--olivia-arcana.netlify.app/ (UK `/uk/`); the build of the tested commit `c19bdeb` is https://6ab820e030b21a0008ba98f9--olivia-arcana.netlify.app/. It works through the gap audit in `../olivia-gap-audit-2026-09-26.md`: speed on phones, the brand around the reading experience, trust and safety, Ukrainian parity, sub-page design, and motion.

## What changed

### Speed on phones

- **Scripts.** The homepage used to load its scripts one after another, so no button worked until the 2.5 MB WebGPU background had downloaded. They now download in parallel and run in order, with the app before the background. The background loads only when the browser has a working WebGPU adapter; otherwise the designed still palette stays and the bundle is never fetched (`NativeExperienceHome.tsx`, `NativeExperienceRuntime.tsx`).
- **Hero art.** On stages narrower than 700 px, `hero.js` draws every card at 512×1024. Those exact textures are now pre-made (`hero-v12/assets/public/cards-portal-phone/`, generated by `experience/work/tools/phone-art.mjs`), so phones download 1.8 MB instead of 5.3 MB. `hero.js` itself is unchanged; the asset map picks the set.
- **Fonts.** The embedded TrueType fonts are now WOFF2: 631 KB becomes 148 KB, with identical glyphs (every table compared). The website no longer preloads the serif and mono fonts on every route (the homepage never used them).
- **Bundles.** esbuild writes UTF-8 instead of escaping 70,000 Cyrillic characters. English pages load an app bundle without the Ukrainian data (`bundle-app.mjs`): English 551 KB, Ukrainian 807 KB (v4: 932 KB for both). The website no longer ships Supabase while accounts are off, and the night-room chrome (smooth scroll, two WebGL grounds, audio, Sky Atlas) is split out of the shared bundle.
- **Lazy previews.** The spread previews in the hidden Spreads view no longer download on the homepage.
- **Caching and hygiene.** One-year immutable caching for hashed files. 113 stale builds (55 MB) removed; the build now prunes old assets.

Measured on a 390×844 phone profile, empty cache, local static server, 5 s without interaction:

| | v4 | v5 EN | v5 UK |
|---|---|---|---|
| After 5 s idle | 8.51 MB | 4.86 MB | 5.14 MB |
| Scripts | 4.34 MB | 1.42 MB | 1.68 MB |
| Images | 3.29 MB | 2.75 MB | 2.75 MB |
| Fonts | 375 KB | 187 KB | 203 KB |
| Transferred by the load event | 2.49 MB | about 1.9 MB | about 1.9 MB |

Byte counts are uncompressed response bodies (Netlify also compresses text). Headless Chromium has no WebGPU adapter, so the shader bundle is not fetched in the v5 columns; on WebGPU devices it still loads, after the app.

### Brand around the product

- Web app manifest, link-preview images (new EN/UK images from the real deck and card back; source `website/scripts/og/og.html`), 404 page, and the Ask page title and description now describe the tarot product.
- The site-wide ground is lapis, not the earlier indigo; checkout panels are no longer violet.
- The "SKY ATLAS · PRESS M" control and the two hidden WebGL canvases appear only on the four earlier night rooms (`/cosmos`, `/oracle`, `/portrait`, `/synastry`).
- Ukrainian pages are exported with `lang="uk"` (`website/scripts/postbuild.mjs`, plus `HtmlLang` for client navigation).
- Gate and placeholder pages (login, register, profile, account, checkout, onboarding, oracle-letter, timing, transits, studies) are marked noindex; the sitemap drops the gate pages and adds `/uk/ask/`; the service worker precaches current pages only; `/animation/` and `/experience/` send `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`.
- `BRAND.md` is the current brand book; the three astrology-era design documents are marked superseded.

### Trust and safety

- **AI label.** Every personal reading says, where it is shown: "Prepared with AI for your question and the cards you chose. It can be mistaken; you decide what fits." (Ukrainian included.) The method page and /about already promised this.
- **Crisis support.** `support-note.js` recognises clear self-harm or danger language (English, Ukrainian and common Russian phrasings; tested against ordinary questions about endings and change). It shows a calm note with 112/911/999, Lifeline Ukraine 7333, Samaritans 116 123, 988 and findahelpline.com, while the question is written and on single and spread readings, whether or not the AI runs.
- **Saving.** Every save asks the browser for persistent storage. On iPhone and iPad (outside the Home Screen app) the almanac explains that Safari may clear saved readings after about a week without a visit, and points to Home Screen and backup.
- **Legal pages.** The privacy policy now describes what actually happens: the almanac on the device, what a personal reading sends to Netlify and Anthropic and when, IP addresses held only in memory, no cookies, no trackers, no accounts or payments. Cookies, terms and refund pages carry corrected current-service text. **These need review by a lawyer**, and the operator named on them (Olivia Arcana LLC, Wyoming) must be confirmed by the owner.
- **Footer.** The homepage footer links to About, Privacy, Terms and Contact.
- **Service.** `/api/reading` and `/api/chat` refuse POSTs without a same-site `Origin` (or `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`). The AI now receives the product's curated card reflections instead of the old library's "the universe supports…" texts.

### Ukrainian parity and reading depth

- `card-notes-uk.js`: native Ukrainian of all 78 curated card notes (meaning, question, practice, symbolism) and all reversals, in the formal «ви» and the product's non-predictive voice. It replaces the old library's informal «ти» texts in the app, on the website's `/uk/cards/` pages and in what the AI receives. Guide: `experience/work/olivia-product/UK-VOICE.md`.
- `minor-reversed.js`: the 56 Minor Arcana reversals were one fill-in-the-blanks sentence; each card now has its own reversed reflection.
- `experience/work/tools/sync-card-notes.mjs` copies the curated EN/UK notes into `website/src/lib/academy/tarot-notes.ts`.
- Card names use the typographic apostrophe (П’ятірка, Дев’ятка).

### Inner pages

- One shared masthead (`AlmanacMasthead.tsx`) for `AlmanacShell` and `LegalShell`. On phones it is about 115 px (was up to 194 px): wordmark and ivory "Begin a reading" on one row, the four links on one scrollable row. The button matches the homepage's.
- The English and Ukrainian card libraries show card art (78 thumbnails, 5 KB each, lazy).
- Fixed: unreadable Tarot Encyclopedia tiles; ivory-on-ivory buttons on sign, course and gate pages; the endless checkout spinner (while payments are off, both checkout pages say membership isn't open and nothing was charged); "to to set"; the unpadded "Explore this card's meaning" row on desktop.
- "Explore the tarot library" now opens the card library instead of the astrology Academy.
- A bilingual error page (`src/app/error.tsx`) replaces a blank screen.

### Motion

- `motion-tokens.css`: `--ease-settle`, `--ease-glide`, `--ease-control`, and press/control/arrive/ritual durations. The CSS now uses three named curves instead of ten (plus one outlier). Values are unchanged, so nothing moved.
- Reveal: after the card turns, one soft pass of light crosses the face as it settles (820 ms). Still a single reveal; hidden under reduced motion.
- Product pages (almanac, Today, spreads, method, membership and the other page-like views) fade in over 320 ms instead of cutting. Opacity only, so fixed controls never shift. The deck, card flights and hero keep their own choreography.

### Tooling

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: product tests, website and service tests, and the static export on every push and pull request. Its first runs on GitHub (push and pull request, commit `c19bdeb`) passed.
