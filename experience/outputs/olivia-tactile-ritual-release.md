# Olivia — tactile card choosing

Preview: https://6ab69ba1c7bd2a00a86a9bc3--olivia-arcana.netlify.app/#spreads

Choose **Preview A little clarity** to try the ritual. The preview tab has been opened directly in the choosing state.

## Experience

- A full-width choosing stage, with larger artwork and the explanation tucked away until the reading.
- Continuous browsing through all 78 shuffled positions, with slider, arrows, and keyboard support.
- Pull a card upward to choose it; a short pull returns it to the fan. Tapping remains supported.
- The lifted card continues from its current pose into the selected set, then the completed set opens into the spread.
- Cards stay face down until an explicit reveal. Three-, five-, and eight-card spreads are supported.
- English and Ukrainian copy, responsive layout, and reduced-motion behavior.
