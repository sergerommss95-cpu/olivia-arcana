# Amielle — six representative card readings

29 September 2026. Editorial prototype accompanying six new artwork studies; the live 78-card catalogue and its readings have not been replaced by this file.

## Editorial intention

**Amielle explores how you love, connect and remain yourself.** The six cards give that promise a varied emotional vocabulary. They preserve the tarot identity of each card while finding a recognisable experience within relationships. Amielle's voice is close, warm and specific; it makes room for longing, pleasure and celebration alongside truth, solitude and endings.

| Card | Relational territory | Tarot identity retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VI · The Lovers | Intimacy freely chosen | Union, choice, values and alignment |
| IX · The Hermit | Private understanding, a way to return | Lantern, introspection, solitude |
| XI · Justice | Reciprocity and agreements | Scales, sword, accountability and truth |
| XV · The Devil | Desire, attachment and agency | Chains, binding, pleasure and the cost of a pattern |
| IV · Four of Wands | Shared belonging and celebration | Four staffs, threshold, home and communal delight |
| VIII · Eight of Cups | Departure that can carry affection | Eight cups, incompleteness and a search beyond the familiar |

## Artwork-to-reading connection

- **Lovers:** Touch carries the choice; free hands preserve distinct selves within intimacy.
- **Hermit:** The open door softens the old isolated-cliff composition without erasing chosen solitude.
- **Justice:** Offerings give reciprocity physical form; the straight marble sword preserves discernment.
- **Devil:** A loose gold chain held in the figures’ own hands leaves agency visible without implying every real constraint is easily escaped. Turned faces and a shared golden pomegranate hold desire beside distance.
- **Four of Wands:** Two people preparing a home meal beneath four olive-adorned staffs give this family of materials everyday warmth.
- **Eight of Cups:** Seven cups on the table plus one carried make eight in total. Carrying one is an intentional Amielle reinterpretation: a meaningful departure can retain memory. The traditional identity of leaving an emotionally incomplete situation remains central.

These scene descriptions are art-direction notes, to be checked against the final generated images before publication. Specific counts, gestures and props should agree with the completed artwork; adjust the note if the final visual differs rather than implying absent details are visible.

## Reading structure

`readings.json` contains six records, each with a stable original card ID, slug and printed number, plus English and Ukrainian title, theme, scene, upright reading, reversed reading and reflection question. English upright passages are roughly 75–85 words; reversed passages are roughly 45–50 words. Ukrainian is written as natural Ukrainian prose, preserving the emotional intention rather than English word order.

The theme gives a short entry into the card. The main reading should sit beside the artwork in a comfortable reading measure. Reversed interpretations and the reflection question can unfold after the main passage. Reversals describe a tension or movement within the card, rather than reducing it to a negative opposite.

These are card-level editorial readings, not question-aware personal synthesis. A later implementation can give the synthesis engine the selected deck and its relational editorial context while continuing to honour the user's exact question, spread positions and actual drawn cards. Do not imply this prototype has already changed that engine or all 78 meanings.

## Voice and inclusion

No gender is assigned to the person asking or their partner in either language. The text makes no claim to know another person's private thoughts or to guarantee a future outcome. The main experience can remain intimate and evocative without repeating technical explanations in every card. Solitude is not treated as a failure of love; pleasure is not shamed; separation is not prescribed by a card. Friendship, chosen family, partnership and a relationship with oneself all have room within the collection.

The user also requested gay and lesbian artwork variants. Lovers, Devil and Four of Wands can offer woman-and-man, two-men and two-women compositions with the **same readings, symbolism and level of intimacy**. These are optional visual choices; do not infer a visitor's orientation from a selection. In particular, The Devil's desire/attachment meaning applies equally to every composition, with no association between orientation and the card's difficult themes. Hermit, Justice and Eight of Cups are shared scenes.

## Review before extending to 78

Review these six for distinct emotional character alongside the images, then carry the accepted editorial rules into the remaining cards. The suits need their own texture: Cups for feeling and emotional exchange; Wands for desire, initiative and shared vitality; Swords for words, truth and mental patterns; Pentacles for time, touch, care and the life built together. Avoid rewriting every card as a version of “communicate and set boundaries.”
