## Design Context

### Users
People arriving at Olivia Arcana for personal astrology and tarot readings, seeking clarity and a reflective ritual. Source: owner-supplied DESIGN_BRIEF.md, interpreted as product context.

### Brand Personality
Serene, monumental, lucid. Preserve the personal-almanac idea and clear writing. Current owner requests a consistent living world matching supplied surreal blue architectural references.

### Aesthetic Direction
Ultramarine sanctuary: grainy blue-and-moonstone lithographic landscape, classical temple, water, mist, generous editorial typography. One restrained gilt accent on the primary action. This is a standalone first-screen proposal, not a production edit.

### Design Principles
1. One scene, one palette, one coherent material language.
2. Reading and primary actions available immediately.
3. Move water and atmosphere; keep architecture steady.
4. Responsive composition, Ukrainian and English parity, visible keyboard focus, motion pause and reduced-motion parity.
5. No invented live celestial data. Decorative landscape is metaphorical.
