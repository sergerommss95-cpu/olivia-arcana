OLIVIA ARCANA — THE OPEN OLIVE

The identity begins with the supplied artwork's sweeping olive bough. The logo
is an original native vector drawing: five tapered leaves, two olives and an
open oval with a serpentine turn. The wordmark is outlined Cormorant Garamond
Medium. See README-logo.txt and FONT-LICENSE.txt for the vector assets.

SCULPTURAL ARTWORK
sculptural-emblem.png — ivory, lapis and gold rendering of the drawn mark.
card-back.png — two opposing branches developed into a continuous reverse.
These raster images were generated with the built-in image generator using
the native drawings and the user's supplied material reference. They are
original tool outputs, not manually upscaled. Full prompts are in prompts.json.

The card artwork is composed for visual balance through a half-turn; it is
not a pixel-exact rotational repeat. This is digital artwork. A physical
print release still needs the printer's trim, bleed and colour specifications.

The gallery is olivia-identity.html. It previews both logo colours, the relief
and the full card, with enlargement, rotation and download controls.

All existing galleries and the website hero are preserved.
