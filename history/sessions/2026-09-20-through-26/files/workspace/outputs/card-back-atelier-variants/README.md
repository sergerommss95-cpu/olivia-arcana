# Olivia Arcana — ten atelier variations

Ten numbered compositions developed from The Fold, with the same slender olive, midnight-blue surface and fine muted-gold finish.

01 The Fold — opposing fans.
02 The Embrace — side folds.
03 Four Corners — four small fans around an open centre.
04 The Inclination — a diagonal pair.
05 The Vault — layered, closed architectural arches.
06 The Aperture — an oval pleated surround.
07 The Current — two continuous flowing ribbons.
08 The Leaf — two botanical folds.
09 The Repeat — an ordered field of small fans around a clear oval.
10 The Solitary Fan — one fan with a lower olive signature.

## Included for each number

- `NN.svg`: self-contained vector master with the digital material finish.
- `NN.png`: 1400 × 2400 preview.
- `NN.webp`: 700 × 1200 lightweight preview.
- `NN-flat.svg`: flat blue-and-gold drawing.
- `NN-foil.svg`: one-colour gold artwork separation.

All ornament and marks are native vector paths. No generated raster plates are embedded. The blue relief and gold lighting are presentation effects; the flat drawings expose the contour system beneath them.

The ratio is 7:12. For physical production, the chosen printer should set bleed and cutting tolerances and proof the smallest foil lines and gaps. These are artwork alternatives for selection; none has been applied to the hero or live website.
