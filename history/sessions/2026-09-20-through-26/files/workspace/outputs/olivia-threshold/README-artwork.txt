OLIVIA ARCANA — THE VEILED THRESHOLD

This identity develops the user's chosen 02 — The Veiled Threshold artwork.
The upright logo abstracts the arch and two parted veils. A companion oval
seal repeats its veil contour through a half-turn for a reversible mark.
See README-logo.txt and FONT-LICENSE.txt for the editable native vectors.

card-back.png — refined double-ended arch, lapis columns and ivory drapery.
sculptural-emblem.png — rendered application of the upright vector logo.
reference-original.png — original artwork selected by the user.
prompts.json — full prompts for the generated artwork.

The native logos are authored vector paths with outlined lettering.
The two relief images were generated using the built-in image generator
with the selected artwork and the native mark as references.

The card illustration is visually balanced through a half-turn, but is not
a pixel-identical rotational repeat. The native oval seal is geometrically
reversible. Physical printing requires trim, bleed and colour proofing to
the chosen printer's specifications.

Open olivia-threshold.html to compare the logo and reverse together.
