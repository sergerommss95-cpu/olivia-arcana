# Olivia Arcana — depth and fine detail

Ten numbered card-back alternatives, created after the earlier set lost too much material depth.

The lapis material plates were created with built-in ImageGen using the actual Moon card as the craftsmanship and palette reference. Exact final prompts are in `prompts.json`. Each finished design then composes the original olive vector paths over that plate as a small gold inlay. The original stem, three leaf silhouettes and oval fruit are preserved. The original app-icon dew highlight is omitted; subtle gold grain, contact shading and internal leaf engraving adapt the mark to this application.

Each SVG master contains its material image and editable vector mark. It is a mixed raster/vector design, not an entirely vector illustration. The matching PNG and WebP files are full-size exports. Original material plates are preserved in the project under `work/material-backs/plates`.

The ten options are for selection. None has been applied to the hero or the live website.
