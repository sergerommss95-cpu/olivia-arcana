## Design Context

### Users
Visitors exploring Olivia Arcana through tarot, then drawing a card as a prompt for reflection. This single-file experience must be usable locally and without a network connection.

### Brand Personality
Contemplative, sculptural, editorial. The supplied brief calls for ivory porcelain, platinum edges, monumental typography, cinematic continuity, and deliberate asymmetry.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm near-black, chalk ivory, restrained antique metal. A compressed deck is the protagonist; cards remain rigid throughout an authored procession, celestial expansion, and layered optical convergence. Avoid pseudo-scientific data, chapter UI, starfield spectacle, generic orbs, and a repeated landing-page template.

### Design Principles
- The same cards carry the complete narrative.
- Make scale, lighting, and occlusion do the work.
- Copy invites reflection without claiming prediction.
- Respect keyboard, touch, reduced motion, and unavailable WebGL.
- One renderer and one scroll spine; all assets embedded.

### Latest visual feedback
The user explicitly rejected the cards and 3D as cheap-looking. Evaluate the rendered surfaces, light, carving and physical depth directly. Passing technical checks does not establish aesthetic success.
