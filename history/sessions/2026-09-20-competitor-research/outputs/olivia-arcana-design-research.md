# Olivia Arcana: competitor and experience research

Researched 20 September 2026. Public pages and selected interactions were inspected. App/account features are official product descriptions unless stated otherwise. Rankings express relevance to Olivia, not traffic or revenue.

## Make the spectacle become the reading.

Competitor research, motion references and an implementation direction for oliviaarcana.com. Reviewed 20 September 2026.

### A living celestial almanac

Preserve Olivia's indigo, ivory and gold world: architectural arches, a lunar figure, reflective water, delicate constellations and editorial typography. Make the opening resolve into a useful sample or reading choice. The site already has a distinctive visual foundation; the next investment should connect that identity to the product.

### Three priorities

**1.** Keep a direct reading action visible from the first viewport, alongside the cinematic exploration.
**2.** Connect the existing daily card, journal and Academy into a single useful loop.
**3.** Give the hero one signature payoff: the portal opens into a readable almanac or a chosen card.

### Scope and confidence

This shortlist ranks relevance and design usefulness, not market share. Public desktop pages and selected interactions were inspected; native-app and account features rely on official descriptions. No payments, personal birth details or journal entries were submitted. Mobile behavior, field performance and conversion rates were not measured. Premium visual impact with a clear reading path is the working priority; audience and commercial goals remain to be confirmed.

## Keep the identity. Improve the connections.

Direct observations of the current public site, rather than assumptions based on older screenshots or project notes.

### What already works

**Distinctive world:** the classical portal, water, restrained palette and almanac language feel authored. The hero already has a staged journey, skip and pause controls. The Oracle exposes four spread choices and optional audio. This is beyond a generic gradient-and-card layout.

### The product is broader than the homepage suggests

The daily page includes a card reveal, sign selector, sky context and a reflection prompt. The journal already offers writing, a calendar, local-device storage, a streak and export. Academy presents 14 courses across astrology, tarot and integrated practice, plus a card encyclopedia. A worked sample reading also exists. Improve these connections before commissioning duplicate features.

[Daily](https://oliviaarcana.com/daily/) | [Journal](https://oliviaarcana.com/journal/) | [Academy](https://oliviaarcana.com/academy/) | [Sample](https://oliviaarcana.com/sample/)

### Priority 1: make the first action unmistakable

At the inspected 1280 x 720 viewport, the initial hero's primary button sat at the lower edge. Its journey action advances the visual sequence rather than directly opening a reading. Keep the cinematic invitation, but add an immediately visible reading action and clarify their different outcomes.

### Priority 2: let discovery reveal value, not hide labels

The Sky Atlas is memorable, but several unvisited destinations appeared visually as Uncharted. Keep their real destination names visible; use stars, trails or stamps to reward visits. Add a readable destination list. After a daily reveal, the generic Academy link should open a lesson about the actual card or concept encountered.

### Priority 3: resolve offer and sample inconsistencies

The sample page's browser title describes a free three-card reading, while its body is a natal portrait. Align the title, preview and next action. Pricing currently says access is open and no payment is taken, while displaying future tiers. Make today's access state explicit wherever future tier labels appear.

[Sample page](https://oliviaarcana.com/sample/) | [Current pricing](https://oliviaarcana.com/pricing/)

### Priority 4: carry the same clarity into the details

Small, widely spaced gold labels and low-contrast secondary text need a readability pass; no measured accessibility failure is claimed. Bring the reading method closer to the experience: About identifies Olivia as a persona and describes AI assistance. Explain that plainly beside readings, distinguishing calculated sky data from symbolic interpretation.

## Four competitors to study first.

These match Olivia's core jobs: explore a reading, understand its relevance, reflect and learn. Features marked app are not necessarily available on the public website.

**Design:** forest green, antique gold, original deck illustration, zodiac linework and large serif type create a consistent visual world.
**Features:** browser and mobile readings, custom spreads, saved readings, notes, flashcard learning and optional AI interpretation. Free access, premium features and deck commerce coexist.
**For Olivia:** unify the card back, symbols and reveal choreography across Oracle, Academy, journal and share images. Attach learning to the reading already in progress.

[Browser experience](https://app.labyrinthos.co/) | [Official app description](https://labyrinthos.co/pages/app)

### 2. CHANI / a reason to return each day

**Design:** graph-paper textures, surreal collage, hand-drawn symbols and an editorial approach give astrology a recognizable voice.
**Features:** web horoscopes, chart tools and education; the app adds personalized chart/transit guidance, audio, meditations, affirmations and journaling prompts.
**For Olivia:** assemble existing sky context, card and prompt into one short daily practice. Add a relevant next action and a clear explanation of how readings are produced.

[Website](https://www.chani.com/) | [App features](https://www.chani.com/app)

**Design:** cream pages, editorial serif headlines, restrained blue and clear product screenshots foreground the benefits.
**Features:** the app describes personal cycles, relationship Bonds, transit exploration through Time Travel, audio and AI conversation (In-Depth); its website offers a natal-chart entry point.
**For Olivia:** structure readings around what the visitor can understand and reflect on. Surface the existing sample; make chart context and the symbolic interpretation separate, expandable layers.

[Website](https://www.thepattern.com/) | [Official features](https://www.thepattern.com/app-features)

### 4. Biddy Tarot / a coherent learning journey

**Design:** plum and lavender, generous space, founder photography and serif headings establish teaching authority.
**Features:** public card draw and meanings, free resources/community, courses and paid membership tiers.
**For Olivia:** route visitors by intention: receive insight or learn the practice. Connect each card to a brief explanation and the next suitable lesson. Show real editorial contributors if available; do not present the Olivia persona as a human founder.

## Three directions, one clear recommendation.

Proposed concepts, not implemented changes. Start with the existing portal; prototype one transition before expanding the production scope.

### A. The Living Almanac / recommended

Keep the existing scene and headline, but frame the figure and arch so a direct reading button and the current access status fit immediately. A separate exploration action advances the scene. The portal then resolves into an open almanac containing a clearly labeled sample insight and a direct path into Oracle.

Arrival Promise + direct CTA → Invitation Optional portal motion → Payoff Readable sample insight → Choice Reading, daily or learn

**Motion script:** a brief light/ripple response acknowledges intent; one coordinated camera or layer movement opens the portal; the page becomes still as the reading becomes available. The exact duration should be tested, not treated as a required wait. Returning visitors get a direct route to today's content.
**Why it fits:** it unites the existing theatrical hero with the existing editorial almanac, instead of introducing a separate visual identity.
**Implementation shape:** start with existing assets plus layered transforms. Add a custom 3D transition only if the simpler prototype fails to achieve the desired effect.

### B. The Card as Portal / product-first alternative

A single richly illustrated card floats within the arch. A click or tap turns it to reveal a complete short sample, followed by a choice of existing spreads. Pointer tilt is subtle; touch and keyboard receive the same reveal.
**Best when:** the primary objective is to get more visitors into their first reading.
**Effort:** medium, depending on whether the existing card system can be reused.

### C. The Personal Orrery / ambitious alternative

An elegant celestial wheel highlights a selected symbol and opens a short explanation. Visitors can explore an explicit demo before choosing to provide their birth details. The visual should label astronomical positions and symbolic meanings separately.
**Best when:** personal chart interpretation is the main offer.
**Effort:** high; interaction design, chart state and mobile performance require dedicated work.

### Visual rules across all three

Keep warm ivory, indigo, restrained gold, literary headings and quiet reading surfaces. Commission a coherent deck and symbol family if the current assets are inconsistent. Favor one large, meaningful motion moment; make labels, navigation and reading text stable. Preserve skip, pause and optional sound.

## Extend what is already there.

P1 = first release; P2 = next product iteration; P3 = evidence-dependent expansion. Effort is relative, pending a code and asset review.

### P1 / A direct route into the existing product / low-medium

Keep a reading action visible at common desktop and mobile sizes. Bring the worked sample into the home journey and align its title with its content. Explain current free access and future memberships plainly. **Success:** a first-time visitor can identify the offer and start a reading without finishing the opening animation.

### P1 / Named Atlas navigation / low-medium

Keep every destination named even before it is visited; let discovery change its styling. Provide a list alongside the map and keep it keyboard-operable. **Success:** a visitor can find the journal or Academy without guessing what an uncharted point contains.

### P1 / One daily loop / medium

The card, sky context, prompts, journal and Academy already exist. Link them: today's card → short interpretation → reflect in journal → lesson related to this card. Carry the relevant card/date into the reflection flow. **Success:** one coherent path replaces the generic jump to the Academy index.

### P2 / Readings that explain themselves / medium

Add expandable explanations for the card's visual symbolism, conventional meaning and relevant chart context. Show the question or topic being addressed. Let people distinguish a general daily card from a personalized interpretation. **Success:** the visitor understands why an element appears in the reading.

### P2 / Better reading history / medium-high

Extend the existing local journal to link reflections with reading/card context and relevant lessons. Add a monthly overview of saved themes if useful. If cross-device sync is introduced, make it an explicit choice consistent with today's local-storage promise. **Success:** a saved reflection can be revisited in context.

### P2 / A learning constellation / medium

Use the Atlas visual language for progress through the existing courses. Highlight the next suitable lesson and offer an ordinary list view. Add small interactive symbol explanations inside lessons before producing more long-form content. **Success:** users know where to begin and where to continue.

### P2-P3 / Shareable insights and richer personal views

Create an elegant share image from a selected insight, with private fields excluded by default. Refine the discoverability and presentation of existing transit, timing and relationship destinations before planning replacements; their deeper flows were not tested in this review. Prioritize audio, custom spreads or community only after checking actual usage.

## Build in three passes. Measure the result.

Planning ranges are rough estimates for a small experienced team reusing current assets. They are not a quotation; a repository and asset review should set scope.

### Pass 1 / clarity and connection / roughly 1-2 weeks

Resolve hero action visibility, sample metadata, Atlas labels and access-state copy. Map the existing routes and connect the daily result to journal and a specific lesson. Establish baseline start/completion rates before changing the cinematic opening.

### Pass 2 / signature hero / roughly 2-4 weeks

Prototype the Living Almanac transition using existing art. Review it on desktop, touch devices and reduced motion. Implement a static fallback and clear direct actions first; then add optional visual depth. Test whether visitors understand what the service provides.

### Pass 3 / repeat-use improvements / roughly 2-4 weeks

Improve saved-reading context, contextual learning and the returning-visitor view. Consider share images and a learning map. A bespoke 3D rebuild or new synced-account system can materially extend these ranges and should be scoped separately.

### Motion and performance acceptance criteria

Keep text and actions available while visual assets load. Pause offscreen animation, use modest scene complexity on touch devices, and provide keyboard equivalents. Respect reduced-motion preferences with a composed static scene or gentle dissolve. Keep sound opt-in.
Use real-user targets at the 75th percentile: **LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP at or below 200 ms, CLS at or below 0.1.** These are recommended targets, not measured results for Olivia. Prefer the existing stack; CSS/SVG may suffice for simple reveals, while GSAP ScrollTrigger is an option for coordinated scroll sequences.

[Google Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) | [Reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-motion) | [ScrollTrigger docs](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/)

### What to measure

**Activation:** homepage → reading start → completed reveal.
**Usefulness:** result → saved reflection or relevant lesson.
**Retention:** daily visitors returning within seven days.
**Commercial clarity:** pricing comprehension; later, real paid conversion once billing is active.
**Experience:** opening skips, device-specific completion, loading and interaction performance. An award-style effect earns its place if it strengthens these outcomes.
