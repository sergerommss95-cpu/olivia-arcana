Quiet Authority, Restless Feet

Build the house, and keep the road in sight.

You are the person a friend calls when something needs to end, and you hang up already working out what comes next. People read you as capable and in charge, and they are right about the capability. What they miss is how much happens in private first: you watch, you turn a thing over for days, and you speak only once the answer feels true to you.

Warm Door, Cool Verdict

You check the mood of a room before you check the agenda. Your first gestures are protective — a coffee offered, a question about someone's mother — and people relax, then expect you to take charge. Behind the warmth your mind has already stepped back to look at the whole arrangement and spotted which part has stopped working.
