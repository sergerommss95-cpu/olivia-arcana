# TUMBLER — production bible

A ~33 s, 24 fps, 2.39:1 (1920x804 inside 1920x1080), fully procedural 3D short about a bank
robbery, made in Blender 5.2 (Cycles, CPU) from Python. No humans, no downloaded models, no image
textures: every object, material, light and camera move is generated by the scripts in `lib/` and
`shots/`. The title is the part of a combination lock that has to line up before the vault opens:
the tumbler wheels.

The heist is told by the objects. One continuous dream that keeps "breaking in" to the next world
through impossible but photographed-looking transitions: lock → vault door → vault → city → the
banknote → back to the lock.

Style source: the look and structure of ESCAPEMENT by Leon Lin (github.com/Leonxlnx/escapement,
MIT). We take the method (procedural Python + headless Blender + code-only edit/grade/mix) and the
STYLE (macro DOF, practical light, wet blacks, teal vs amber, grain, halation, match cuts). We do
not reuse its models, scenes or edit.

## Story / acts (frames @24 fps; music grid 12 frames = 1 beat, 48 frames = 1 bar)

I   THE COMBINATION — macro lock (shots/act1_lock.py, lib/lock.py)
    1A  Black. A thin light sweeps across a steel combination dial at a grazing angle: knurled
        rim, engraved ticks, cream-filled numerals. The dial whips round and stops dead on a
        number with a tiny bounce.
    1B  Inside the lock case: brass tumbler wheels on a spindle, each with a gate notch. Lateral
        dolly + rack focus as the gates swing into line one by one on the beat.
    1C  The fence drops into the gates, the bolt bar slams back. Camera ends head-on down the
        spindle axis on concentric circles  ==> T1 match cut.
II  THE DOOR — vault antechamber (shots/act2_door.py, lib/vault.py)
    2A  Head-on on the vault door's central hub (matches 1C) → fast pull back: a 3 m round vault
        door in a dark concrete room, wet floor, cool fluorescent strips. The spoked hand wheel
        spins.
    2B  Low along the door rim: the chrome locking bolts retract in a ripple, 4 groups on 4 beats.
    2C  The 20-tonne door swings open; warm light and haze pour through the gap; the camera
        pushes through the door's metre-thick edge into the vault.
III THE VAULT (shots/act3_vault.py)
    3A  FPV glide down an aisle of safe-deposit boxes; red laser beams cross a dusty haze; the
        camera banks to thread between them toward a stack of gold.
    3B  The deposit-box doors fly open in a wave down the wall (impossible), cash bundles inside.
    3C  Macro across stamped gold bars, a slow light sweep, edges catching light.
    3D  Head-on on the wall of boxes, pulling back until it is a perfect grid  ==> T3.
IV  THE GETAWAY — night city (shots/act4_city.py, lib/city.py)
    4A  The grid is now the lit window grid of an office tower at night (same layout). The camera
        drops down the facade to a wet street; a black car punches out of the bank's garage ramp,
        red/blue light washing the walls.
    4B  Camera on the asphalt: the car charges the lens and passes inches away, banknotes
        swirling in its wake.
    4C  Tracking alongside at speed: police-light reflections slide over black paint, street
        lights streak, notes stream from the rear window.
    4D  Rear chase into a tunnel; one banknote tumbles toward the lens and fills the frame ==> T4.
V   THE ROSETTE (shots/act5_title.py)
    5A  Macro of the banknote's guilloche rosette, turning — it match-dissolves into the dial face
        from 1A (same circle). The dial clicks to 0.
    5B  Title "TUMBLER" machined in steel, tumbler wheels turning behind, light sweep, to black.

## Transition handoffs

- T1 1C end → 2A start: both head-on down a rotation axis, concentric rings centred, outer ring
  ~90% of frame height, warm brass centre vs cool steel ring.
- T2 inside 2C (one continuous push through the opening door).
- T3 3D end → 4A start: deposit-box grid head-on, 7 columns x 4 rows visible; 4A opens on the tower
  facade with the same 7x4 window grid. Edit: 6-frame luma dissolve (bright box plates → lit
  windows).
- T4 4D end → 5A start: the banknote fills frame flat; 5A starts flat on the same note.
- T5 inside 5A (rosette → dial dissolve in the edit).

## Look rules (all acts)

- Compose for 2.39:1 — render the band only (1280x536, upscaled in post).
- Every frame: one clear focal subject, depth layers (foreground occluder / subject / background),
  atmosphere (haze, dust, rain mist), deep blacks, small bright speculars, motivated light.
- Detail density is the game: bevels on every hard edge, machined/brushed finishes, screws,
  rivets, engraved numerals, panel gaps, per-object colour/roughness variation.
- Effects must look photographed: dust is sparse, tiny, out of focus; laser beams are thin and
  only visible where haze is; banknotes are paper (bend, flutter, motion blur), never confetti.
- Never too bright. Most of the frame in shadow and rich mid-tones; highlights are small. Lights
  are coloured and moderate. QC every still with tools/exposure_check.py.
- Palette: Act I warm brass vs cool steel/cyan haze; Act II cold fluorescent, wet black, amber
  spill from the vault; Act III warm gold vs red lasers and teal shadows; Act IV sodium orange vs
  police red/blue on wet black; Act V cream paper, steel, brass.
- Camera moves are motivated and fast: whip pans, speed ramps (ease in/out keys), handheld micro
  shake (common.shake), rack focus. Motion blur ON. Anamorphic oval bokeh (aperture ratio 1.6).

## Technical rules

- Machine: 4 CPU cores, 15 GB RAM, no GPU. Cycles CPU + OpenImageDenoise. ONE Blender process at a
  time — always render through `tools/rb.py` (file lock).
- Test stills: `python3 tools/rb.py shots/<act>.py -- --shot 1A --stills 1,24 --pct 50`.
  Motion check: `--anim --jpg --pct 35 --samples 8 --step 3 --outdir tests/<shot>_motion` then
  `python3 tools/sheet.py tests/<shot>_motion`.
- Final budget: <= 20 s per frame at 1280x536. Volumes kept local and thin.
- Each act script: `--shot <ID>` selects the scene state, camera and frame range; frames are local
  to the shot starting at 1 and include 6-frame handles at each end (see shotlist.json).
- Output of `finish(shot)`: `out/renders/<ID>/####.png` (16-bit).
- Edit, grade and mix are code: `tools/edit.py` (numpy + ffmpeg), `tools/mix.py`.
- Sound: only real free assets (Mixkit license / CC0). Files are not committed; see
  `audio/manifest.json` and `tools/fetch_audio.py`.
