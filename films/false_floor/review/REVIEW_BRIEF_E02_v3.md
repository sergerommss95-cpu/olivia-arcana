### What to look for

- **3D renders and stills (RENDER, ANIM):** limbs, hands or fingers bending wrongly; feet sliding or floating; bodies passing through clothes, props, doors or walls; clothing that pops or swims; faces that look broken or frozen; props not held properly; objects appearing or vanishing; flicker, noise or fireflies; texture crawl; a light that jumps; motion that stutters, freezes or pops for one frame; a frozen last frame at the end of a shot.
- **Kling AI clips (AI):** hands with wrong finger counts; faces or objects that morph; text-like gibberish; things melting into each other; physics that looks wrong; a character whose face or clothes differ from the cast sheet; identifiable real people.
- **Graphics (GFX, FACT):** spelling; wrong names, dates or numbers; text too small, cut off, overlapping or off-screen; an element that appears after the narration has moved on; animation that stops dead before the cut.
- **Photos (PHOTO):** any identifiable face; a missing or unreadable credit line; warping from the parallax effect (bent straight lines, smeared edges).
- **Edit (EDIT):** a cut that lands noticeably early or late against the word it belongs to; picture that contradicts the narration; a shot held too long or flashed too briefly to read; the same shot repeated too often (section 8 lists the repeats); a jarring jump between two shots.
- **Grade and labels (GRADE, LABEL):** a shot much darker or brighter than its neighbours; a colour cast; labels missing, doubled, or covering something important.
- **Sound (AUDIO):** dropouts, clicks, clipped or distorted speech; music or effects drowning the voice; an effect out of sync with the action; an abrupt music start or stop; narration that does not match the script in section 6.
