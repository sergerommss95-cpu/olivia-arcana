### Fact checks applied to the Gemini rewrite (not used, and why)
- "a crooked *Jewish* diamond dealer": no source states this and it echoes an antisemitic trope; reported as "a diamond dealer".
- "10,000,000 combinations": wrong (four numbers 0–99 would be 100,000,000, and real dials have tolerance); no number shown.
- Invented specifics removed: a $100,000 sensor vs a $3 can, "woman's hairspray", a keycard, "late 2000", cell-tower records,
  emerald fragments, gold bars on the floor, "zero fingerprints / alarm logs", a 70-year-old Van Camp, "almost every morning",
  "a clean DNA profile handed police their names", detectives "set a trap", "Today he is a free man", "vanish into the dawn".
- Kept as attributed (*he says / according to Notarbartolo*): Speedy's panic, the burn plan, the hidden camera, the insurance-fraud story.
