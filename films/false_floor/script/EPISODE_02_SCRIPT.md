## 3 · TEN LAYERS (3:30–6:00)  *(the big 3D chapter)*

**[3:40] 3D** — Macro: the combination wheel, numbers 0 to 99; a tiny lens on top showing the digit.
> Then the combination. A wheel numbered zero to ninety-nine. Four numbers, dialled in the right order — and the digits can only be read through a small lens on top of the dial, so nobody standing behind you can see what you're doing. [10]

**[3:55] 3D** — Drill bit approaching the door edge; frame freezes before contact.

**[4:10] 3D** — Two steel plates across the door seam, a faint field drawn as fine cream lines that hold (no neon).

**[4:28] 3D** — The key: a long, two-part key turning in a deep lock (macro).

**[6:28] 3D** — The magnetic plates: loose screws, tape; an aluminium plate slid over them; the field holds.

**[6:48] 3D** — A polystyrene sheet carried in front of a figure-shaped absence (no people shown: just the shield moving through the room).

## 8 · TWO STORIES (11:30–13:30)  *(the twist)*

**[11:30] MG** — Split frame: left cream panel **HIS STORY**, right charcoal panel **THE EVIDENCE**.
> In 2009, Notarbartolo gives a long interview to the journalist Joshua Davis for WIRED. It's the first time he tells his side. And his version is extraordinary. [33]

**[11:45] MG** (left) — Typeset claims building line by line, attributed: *"HE SAYS…"*
> He says he wasn't the mastermind at all. He says a diamond dealer hired the crew to stage a robbery for the insurance money. He says they practised in a replica vault so they could work in the dark. He says that when they opened the bags, most of them were nearly empty — that the dealers had quietly moved their stones out before the weekend. Twenty million, he says. Not a hundred. [33]

**[12:15] MG** (right) — Each claim gets a cream counter-line; the light sensor with tape (3D insert); bags on the vault floor (3D insert).
> The investigators' answer is short, and it's built on the crime scene. The thieves didn't work in the dark — the lights were on, and there was tape over the light sensor. The bags weren't empty — the loot was sorted right there on the vault floor, with jewels and passports left behind. [22][35]

**[12:40] MG** — Quote card, full frame, cream on charcoal:
> **"Not one detail of Leo's story can be confirmed by the facts in the investigation."** — Agim De Bruycker [34]

**[12:55] MG** — Both panels slide apart (false floor reveal) to show an empty orange space between them.
> So what is the story beneath? A fraud dressed up as a robbery — with a thief as the fall guy? Or a thief, years later, dressing himself up as a pawn so nobody asks where the diamonds went? Remember: no one has ever proven who else was behind it. And the man telling the story is the one who was convicted of it. [33][34]
